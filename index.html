Signs your East Valley home needs a panel upgrade

You may need a panel upgrade if your breakers trip when the AC and kitchen run together, lights flicker under load, the panel cover feels warm, or a home inspection flagged capacity concerns. Arizona extreme heat also plays into the panels breaking down over time. Years of summer heat in a garage or on an outside wall can loosen connections and wear out breakers.

Many Queen Creek, Gilbert, San Tan Valley, Chandler, Mesa, Scottsdale, Phoenix, and Florence homes still run on original capacity that fit the day they were built, not today's heat loads and add ons. Planning a Level 2 EV charger, kitchen remodel, pool equipment, shop circuits, or an ADU often triggers the need for more capacity.

This is a diagnostic check, not a scare pitch. Next Phase Electric starts with an on site load check so you know whether a repair, a breaker change, or a full upgrade is the honest path.

What a panel upgrade includes

Load assessment

We look at your current amperage, available breaker spaces, and what you plan to add. That list drives the size of the panel, not a one size fits all sales chart.

Right sized equipment

When load requires it, a common path is moving from 100 amp service to 200 amp service. We size to your home, not a push.

Whole home surge protector included

Every panel install includes a whole home surge protector at no extra charge. It helps guard your AC, appliances, and electronics from power surges.
